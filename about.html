My professional profile

Making information accessible.

My experience spans higher education, public-interest communication, media, health, science, agriculture, sport, disability and innovation. I develop institutional content, report on programmes and achievements, support campaigns, and communicate with diverse stakeholders.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Communication Studies and a Postgraduate Diploma in Science Communication from the University of Limpopo, both completed in 2025.

Science, told with clarity.

As a Marketing Liaison Officer in Science Communication & Innovation at the University of Limpopo, I translate technical, scientific and institutional information into accessible stories. My work includes articles, newsletters, reports, social media content and communication around research, intellectual property and entrepreneurship.

A foundation in media.

At Energy FM, I researched and produced news, interviews, features and current-affairs content. Working with presenters and production teams strengthened my interviewing, reporting and audience-focused storytelling in a deadline-driven environment.

My university sports communication experience added a focus on student development, disability inclusion, wellness and the people behind sporting achievements.
